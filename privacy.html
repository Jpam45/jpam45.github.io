Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Bill’s Sudoku does not collect any personal data. There is no account to create, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not send anything over the internet.

作者Who makes Bill’s Sudoku

Bill’s Sudoku is made by Paul Allan Manacap, an independent developer. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the developer of Bill’s Sudoku. This policy covers the Bill’s Sudoku app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and for Android.

端末What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your device. They never leave your device and we cannot see them.

WhatWhy
The name you enterTo greet you and cheer you on when you solve a puzzle
Your current and saved gameSo you can pick up where you left off
Your settingsMusic on or off, kanji numbers, marking wrong numbers, clearing notes
How many puzzles you have solvedTo show your count after each solve

On Android, the app also keeps the date your free trial started and whether you own the full game, so the game knows what to unlock even when you’re offline.

You can change your name at any time from the round name button in the top corner of the game. Deleting the app removes all of this from your device. If your device backs up apps (iCloud backup, or Android backup to your Google Account), these settings can be included in that backup, which is stored by Apple or Google for you and never sent to us.

購入Free trial and purchases

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, the free 30-day trial and the full game are in-app purchases made through Apple’s App Store, and Apple handles the payment. On Android, the full game is bought through Google Play, and Google handles the payment. We never receive your payment details, your Apple Account or Google Account details, or your contact information.

To unlock content, the app checks the purchase records that Apple or Google keep for your account, through the App Store or the Play Store app on your device. This check happens on your device, and the records are not sent to us. Apple’s handling of your purchases is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy, and Google’s by the Google Privacy Policy.

共有Sharing, selling and tracking

  • We do not collect personal data, so we have none to share or sell.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

子供Children

Bill’s Sudoku is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

削除Keeping and deleting data

We keep no data about you, so there is nothing for us to store, keep or delete. Everything the app saves stays on your device, and you can remove it at any time by deleting the app. If you have a question about your data, email us and we will answer it.

変更Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
